Use of Artificial Intelligence & Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

✨ Use of Artificial Intelligence

Our service uses an artificial intelligence (AI) system to understand your messages and generate automated replies. Please note:

  • Message Processing: Your messages are sent to the AI system only to generate a reply to you.
  • No AI Model Training: The AI system does not retain your personal data for its own use, and your data is not used to train AI models.
  • Purpose Limitation: The AI is used only for the purpose of helping you book your appointment and answering your inquiries.

Sharing Notice: We do not share your information with third parties, except service providers that host the service on our behalf (such as Amazon Web Services), the AI service providers described above, and Meta Platforms, which carries the messages.

Privacy Policy Overview

La Cassia Clinica (“we”, “us”) operates an automated messaging service on Facebook Messenger that helps you book an appointment. This policy explains how we handle your information.

What We Collect

When you message our Page, we receive your Facebook name, your Page-scoped ID (PSID), and the details you send us in the conversation (such as your name, phone number, and preferred appointment time).

How We Use It

  • The information you provide is used only to book and manage your appointment.
  • Your data is stored the same way as your regular Page messages, as part of your conversation with our Page.
  • We do not use your data for any other purpose.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or share it with anyone for marketing.
  • We will never use your information for anything else without your explicit permission.

Data Sharing

We do not share your information with third parties, except service providers that host the service on our behalf (such as Amazon Web Services), AI service providers used to generate automated responses, and Meta Platforms, which carries the messages.

Security

We protect your data with encrypted connections (HTTPS) and restricted access, and we take reasonable technical and organizational measures to keep it safe.

Retention and Deletion

We keep your data only as long as needed to manage your appointment. You can ask us to delete it at any time by emailing privacy@lacassia.tech (or info@lacassia.tech) with the subject “Data Deletion Request” and your Facebook name. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.
